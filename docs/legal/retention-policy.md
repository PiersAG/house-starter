# Retention policy

> This document is a template. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified person before the app takes its first customer.

How long [app name] keeps each kind of record, and what happens to it when an account is deleted.

## Retention periods

Produced from config/personal-data.ts. The retention job (lib/privacy/retention-job.ts) runs every day: it deletes rows older than their age limit, erases accounts whose deletion is due and deletes retained records whose period has ended. Each step is written to the audit trail.

- tenants (Your account details): While your account is open. On account deletion: deleted.
- users (Your account details): While your account is open, then 30 days after you ask us to delete it. On account deletion: deleted.
- revokedSessions (Sign-in security records): 90 days. Rows older than 90 days are deleted. On account deletion: deleted.
- subscriptions (Your subscription): While your account is open. On account deletion: deleted.
- stripeEvents (Payment service records): 90 days. Rows older than 90 days are deleted. On account deletion: nothing to erase.
- billingInvoices (Your invoices): While your account is open; after it is deleted, the amounts and dates for 6 years. On account deletion: only stripeInvoiceId, status, amountPaid, amountDue, currency, paidAt kept for 6 years (UK tax records: 6 years (HMRC record-keeping)), then deleted.
- billingDisputes (Payment disputes): While your account is open; after it is deleted, the amounts and dates for 6 years. On account deletion: only stripeDisputeId, amount, currency, reason, status, openedAt, closedAt kept for 6 years (UK tax and accounting records: 6 years), then deleted.
- passwordResetTokens (Sign-in security records): 30 days. Rows older than 30 days are deleted. On account deletion: deleted.
- settingDefinitions (App settings catalogue): Not personal data. On account deletion: nothing to erase.
- operatorSettingValues (Operator settings): Not personal data. On account deletion: nothing to erase.
- errorEvents (Error records): 30 days. Rows older than 30 days are deleted. On account deletion: nothing to erase.
- credentialEvents (Security records): 1 year. Rows older than 365 days are deleted. On account deletion: kept without ip, tenantId.
- logEvents (Request records): 90 days. Rows older than 90 days are deleted. On account deletion: kept without userId, tenantId, fields.
- auditEvents (Audit trail of account changes): 6 years; it cannot be changed or deleted before then. On account deletion: kept for 6 years: The audit trail is the evidence that the app followed its own rules, deletion included; it is append-only by design (#796)..
- tenantAccessEvents (Security records): 1 year. Rows older than 365 days are deleted. On account deletion: kept without userId, sessionTenantId.
- accessGrants (Your subscription): While your account is open. On account deletion: deleted.
- userMfaTotp (Sign-in security records): While your account is open. On account deletion: deleted.
- userMfaRecoveryCodes (Sign-in security records): While your account is open. On account deletion: deleted.
- retainedRecords (Records kept after deletion): Until the expiry date each record carries (6 years for tax records). On account deletion: nothing to erase.
- erasures (Records kept after deletion): As long as backups that predate the deletion exist; the ids name nobody once the account is gone. On account deletion: nothing to erase.
- tenantOrphans (Databases waiting to be deleted): Until the database is deleted; the id names nobody, as its account is gone. On account deletion: nothing to erase.
- emailEvents (Email delivery reports): 90 days. Rows older than 90 days are deleted. On account deletion: nothing to erase.
- tenantMeta (Your own data): While your account is open. On account deletion: the account's own database is wiped and removed.
- settingValues (Your own data): While your account is open. On account deletion: the account's own database is wiped and removed.
- recordEvents (Your own data): While your account is open. On account deletion: the account's own database is wiped and removed.

## Account deletion

Deactivated at once; erased 30 days later, checked and confirmed by email within 72 hours of the due time. /api/health reports any account still not erased 72 hours after it was due.

## Backups

The account's own database is removed with its restore history where the database platform allows it (the platform key needs its delete permission); otherwise it is left empty. The shared account database's restore points age out within its restore window. After any restore, run `npm run privacy:replay-erasures -- <ledger.json>` with the erasure ledger exported before the restore (`npm run privacy:export-ledger`), before the app reopens.

## The audit trail

The audit trail (audit_events) cannot be changed or deleted by the app: it is the evidence that the policy was followed. Its rows are kept 6 years. Removing rows older than that needs a maintenance change that lifts the append-only rule for those rows; the first are due in 2032, and the operator schedules it then.
