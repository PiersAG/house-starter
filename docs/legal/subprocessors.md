## Vercel

- Status: In use
- What it does: Hosts the app and runs it: every page and request passes through it.
- Personal data it receives: Everything you send to the app passes through it; its request logs hold your IP address and the pages you open for a short time.
- Where: United States and EU edge locations (the app's functions run in the region set for the project).
- Transfers out of the UK: UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, in Vercel's DPA.
- Data processing agreement: https://vercel.com/legal/dpa
- On account deletion: Request logs expire on their own within the plan's log window; nothing else is stored there.

## Turso

- Status: In use
- What it does: The app's databases: your account and your own data.
- Personal data it receives: Your account details and everything you keep in the app.
- Where: The region chosen for the app's database group.
- Transfers out of the UK: UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, in Turso's DPA.
- Data processing agreement: https://turso.tech/privacy-policy
- On account deletion: Your own database is deleted, with its restore history; your rows in the shared account database are deleted or anonymised.

## Upstash

- Status: In use
- What it does: Counts requests so the app can limit abuse (rate limits).
- Personal data it receives: Your IP address and account id, as counters that expire within a day.
- Where: The region chosen for the app's Redis database.
- Transfers out of the UK: UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, in Upstash's DPA.
- Data processing agreement: https://upstash.com/trust/dpa.pdf
- On account deletion: Every counter expires on its own, within 24 hours.

## Stripe

- Status: In use
- What it does: Takes subscription payments.
- Personal data it receives: Your email address, name and payment details, which you give to Stripe directly on its payment page; the app never sees your card.
- Where: United States, with EU and UK entities.
- Transfers out of the UK: UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, in Stripe's DPA.
- Data processing agreement: https://stripe.com/legal/dpa
- On account deletion: Stripe keeps payment records under its own legal duties as a financial service; the app's own copy of invoices is kept only as long as tax law requires.

## Amazon Web Services

- Status: In use
- What it does: Holds the app's own keys and passwords in a locked store (the key vault).
- Personal data it receives: None: it holds the app's keys, never customer data.
- Where: EU (London or Ireland region chosen for the vault).
- Transfers out of the UK: Not applicable: no personal data is sent.
- Data processing agreement: https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/gdpr-center/
- On account deletion: Not applicable: no personal data is held.
